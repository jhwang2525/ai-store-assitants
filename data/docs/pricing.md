# Plans & Pricing

## Free plan
The Free plan includes up to 2 devices, 100 MB of storage, and unlimited notes. File attachments are limited to 5 MB each.

## Pro plan
Pro costs $6 per month or $60 per year. It includes unlimited devices, 10 GB of storage, 50 MB attachments, version history for 30 days, and offline access on mobile.

## Team plan
Team costs $10 per user per month, billed yearly, with a minimum of 3 users. It adds shared notebooks, admin controls, single sign-on (SSO), and version history for 1 year.

## Student discount
Students and teachers get 50% off Pro with a valid school email address. The discount is checked again every 12 months.

## Refunds
Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep Pro until the end of the billing period.

## Changing or cancelling a plan
Go to Settings > Billing to upgrade, downgrade or cancel. If you downgrade to Free and use more than 100 MB, your notes stay readable but you cannot add new attachments until you are under the limit.
