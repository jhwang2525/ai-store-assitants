# Getting Started with Nimbus Notes

## What is Nimbus Notes?
Nimbus Notes is a note-taking app for desktop, web and mobile. Notes are written in Markdown, organized into notebooks, and synced across all your devices.

## Supported platforms
Nimbus Notes runs on Windows 10 or later, macOS 12 or later, iOS 16 or later, Android 10 or later, and in any modern web browser at app.nimbusnotes.example. Linux is supported through the web app only.

## Creating your account
Sign up with an email address or with Google. You must verify your email within 7 days, or the account is paused until you verify it.

## Notebooks and tags
Every note lives in exactly one notebook. Tags are optional and a note can have up to 20 tags. You can nest notebooks up to 3 levels deep.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- New note: Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on Mac)
- Search all notes: Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac)
- Toggle Markdown preview: Ctrl+Shift+P (Cmd+Shift+P on Mac)
