# Troubleshooting

## Notes are not syncing
First check that you are online and signed in to the same account on both devices. Then go to Settings > Sync and click "Sync now". If a note still does not appear after 10 minutes, sign out and sign back in; local notes are kept.

## The app is slow with large notebooks
Notebooks with more than 5,000 notes can feel slow on older devices. Split large notebooks, or turn off "Show note previews" in Settings > Appearance.

## I can't sign in
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page to get a reset link. Reset links expire after 1 hour. If you signed up with Google, use "Continue with Google" instead of a password.

## Attachment upload failed
Check the attachment size limit for your plan: 5 MB on Free and 50 MB on Pro and Team. Executable files such as .exe and .bat cannot be uploaded.

## Contacting support
Email support@nimbusnotes.example. Pro and Team users get a reply within 24 hours on business days; Free users within 3 business days. Include your app version from Settings > About.
