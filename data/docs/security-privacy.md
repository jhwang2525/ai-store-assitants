# Security & Privacy

## Encryption
All notes are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Pro and Team users can turn on end-to-end encryption for individual notebooks; for those notebooks, not even Nimbus staff can read the content.

## End-to-end encryption password
The end-to-end encryption password is never sent to Nimbus. If you forget it, the encrypted notebooks cannot be recovered. Write it down or store it in a password manager.

## Two-factor authentication
You can turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) in Settings > Security using an authenticator app. SMS codes are not supported. Team admins can require 2FA for all members.

## Data location
Data for accounts created in the European Union is stored in Frankfurt, Germany. All other accounts are stored in Oregon, USA.

## Exporting and deleting your data
You can export all notes as Markdown files in a ZIP from Settings > Account > Export. Deleting your account removes all data from our servers within 30 days.

## AI features and your data
Nimbus does not use your notes to train AI models.
