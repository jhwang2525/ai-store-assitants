# Sync & Offline Mode

## How sync works
Changes sync automatically within a few seconds when you are online. Each device keeps a local copy of your notes.

## Offline access
Desktop apps work fully offline on every plan. Offline access on iOS and Android requires the Pro or Team plan. Changes made offline sync the next time the device connects.

## Sync conflicts
If the same note is edited on two devices while offline, Nimbus keeps both versions. The second version is saved as a copy named "Note title (conflict)" so nothing is lost.

## Version history
Pro keeps every saved version of a note for 30 days and Team keeps them for 1 year. Open a note, click the clock icon, and choose a version to restore. The Free plan does not include version history.

## Deleted notes
Deleted notes go to Trash and stay there for 30 days on every plan. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered, even by support.
